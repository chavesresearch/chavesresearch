### 👨‍💻 About me

- 🎓 Predoctoral researcher (FPU fellow) at the **University of Málaga**, M.Sc. in Software Engineering & AI.
- 🔬 My PhD focuses on **machine learning over real-time data streams** for IoT and Industry 4.0.
- 🌐 Working on **federated learning**, its decentralisation with **blockchain**, and **model compression for TinyML** on the edge.
- 🧩 Core contributor to **[Kafka-ML](https://github.com/ertis-research/kafka-ml)**, an open-source framework to manage ML/AI pipelines through data streams.
- ✈️ Research stays at **Yonsei University** (Seoul, 2024) and **Ghent University – imec IDLab** (2025).
- 👨‍🏫 Teaching Software Engineering at UMA.
- 🏎️ Off the clock: F1, sports, video games and time with friends.
